# Annotated starter reading list

## Writing instruction / primary
- Judith C. Hochman & Natalie Wexler, *The Writing Revolution 2.0* — sentence-to-essay explicit instruction.
- Ruth Culham, *6+1 Traits of Writing* — assessment and feedback dimensions.
- Oxford University Press, *Oxford International Primary English* — levelled international-school materials (confirm edition and level).
- CGP English writing workbooks — UK year-level exercises (select by demonstrated proficiency).

## Narrative writing
Narrative writing is covered in our Kindle book, *インターナショナルスクールの英語作文がわかる本：Narrative Writing編* (in preparation). See the [book page](../books/narrative-writing.md).

## Academic writing / research
- Gerald Graff & Cathy Birkenstein, *They Say / I Say* — argument and response.
- John M. Swales & Christine B. Feak, *Academic Writing for Graduate Students* — disciplinary academic communication.
- Wayne C. Booth et al., *The Craft of Research* — inquiry and argument.

## Presentation
- Garr Reynolds, *Presentation Zen* — visual storytelling and slide design.
